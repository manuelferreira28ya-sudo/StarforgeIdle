Starforge Idle is a local-only casual idle game. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no account system, no tracking, no external web content, and no user-generated content in this build.

Core flow:
1. Open the app on the Forge tab.
2. Tap the forge core to earn stardust.
3. Claim the featured goal after 10 taps.
4. Buy generator lines and upgrades.
5. Open Goals, Crew, and Supply tabs to review progression.
6. When enough total stardust is earned, use Build > Launch Run. A confirmation sheet explains what resets and what persists.

Privacy manifest declares UserDefaults for local game progress only.
