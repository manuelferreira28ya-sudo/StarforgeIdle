Starforge Idle is a compact cosmic idle game built around quick taps, steady upgrades, and satisfying long-run growth.

Start by waking the forge, then turn stardust into production lines, boosters, crew unlocks, daily supplies, offline earnings, and launch runs that reset your forge for permanent prism power.

Features:
- Tap-to-earn forge core
- Eight generator tiers with milestone boosts
- Ten upgrade branches for taps, production, and offline earnings
- Quest chain that guides progression
- Crew collection with permanent output bonuses
- Offline earnings and daily streak rewards
- Prestige launches for permanent prism growth

No ads, no purchases, no tracking, and no account required in this build.
